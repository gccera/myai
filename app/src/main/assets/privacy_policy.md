# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 25 November 2026

This app ("the App") is an offline AI assistant. All artificial-intelligence processing happens
**on your device**. This policy explains what that means for your data.

## 1. Summary

- **No data leaves your device.** Your chats, prompts, generated images, imported files and
  settings are stored only in the App's private storage on your phone.
- **No account, no servers, no cloud AI.** The App has no backend and does not call any paid or
  cloud AI API.
- **No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.** The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting,
  advertising or tracking SDKs.

## 2. Network use

The App uses the internet **only to download AI model files** that you choose in the Model
Manager. Downloads come directly from Hugging Face (huggingface.co and its content-delivery
network). Like any web download, Hugging Face receives standard technical information such as your
IP address and the file requested; this is governed by Hugging Face's own privacy policy. If you
enter an optional Hugging Face access token (needed only for some licence-gated models), it is
stored on your device and sent only to Hugging Face with those downloads.

No chat text, prompt, image or personal information is ever included in these requests.

## 3. Data stored on your device

| Data | Where | How to delete |
|---|---|---|
| Conversations and messages | App-private database | Delete a chat, or Settings → Clear data |
| Generated images | App-private storage | Delete in Gallery, or Settings → Clear data |
| Downloaded models | App-private storage | Model Manager → Delete |
| Settings (theme, sampling, token) | App-private storage | Settings → Clear data |
| Content reports | App-private database | Settings → Clear data |

Uninstalling the App deletes all of the above. The App disables Android cloud backup and
device-to-device transfer so this data is not copied to Google Drive or other devices.

## 4. Sharing that you start

Data leaves the App only when **you** explicitly choose to:
- **Share** a message or image (Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick);
- **Save to gallery** (the image is copied to your phone's public Pictures folder);
- **Send a report by email** (your email app opens with a pre-filled message that you can review
  before sending).

## 5. Permissions

- **Internet / network state:** model downloads only.
- **Notifications / foreground service:** to show download progress while a model downloads.
- **Storage (Android 8–9 only):** to save images to your gallery when you ask.

## 6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal data
from anyone.

## 7. AI-generated content

AI output can be inaccurate or inappropriate. The App filters prompts and replies against a list
of blocked terms and lets you report any reply or image from within the App. Reports are stored
on your device; you may optionally email a report to the developer.

## 8. Changes and contact

We will update this policy in the App and at the URL listed on our store page if anything changes.
Questions: see the contact e-mail on the App's Google Play listing.
